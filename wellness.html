Breathing Exercises

Simple, evidence-based techniques to calm anxiety, reduce stress, and reset your mind.

4-7-8

Relaxing Breath

Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Ideal for anxiety and exam stress.

BOX

Box Breathing

Inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Used by Navy SEALs to stay calm under pressure.

5-5

Deep Belly Breath

Slow inhale for 5, slow exhale for 5. Activates the body's natural calm response.

Managing Exam Stress

Evidence-based strategies for students facing KCSE, KCPE, and other high-stakes assessments.

  • Create a structured study timetable
  • Take regular breaks (Pomodoro: 25 min work, 5 min rest)
  • Talk to someone you trust about your worries
  • Exercise for at least 20 minutes daily
  • Prioritise 7–9 hours of sleep
🧑‍🏫

Teacher Burnout Prevention

Practical self-care strategies for educators managing large workloads and emotional demands.

  • Set firm work-life boundaries
  • Connect with colleagues for peer support
  • Practice mindfulness for 10 min daily
  • Celebrate small classroom wins
  • Seek supervision or mentorship regularly
👨‍👩‍👧

Supporting Your Child's Mental Health

A guide for parents on recognising signs of distress and fostering open communication.

  • Listen actively without judgment
  • Look out for changes in behaviour or sleep
  • Avoid comparing children to peers
  • Create a safe, calm home environment
  • Know when to seek professional help
🌈

Building Resilience

Develop the mental strength to bounce back from challenges and setbacks.

  • Reframe setbacks as learning opportunities
  • Build a strong social support network
  • Maintain a gratitude journal
  • Develop a growth mindset
  • Find purpose and meaning in daily activities
